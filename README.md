# Routing Atlas

**An open research library for documented findings, technical comparisons, and unresolved questions.**

Routing Atlas organizes research into concise, traceable records. Each finding includes a factual summary, a reference to its source, relevant version or publication details, an inspection date, and questions that remain open.

The current collection focuses on AI systems, software architecture, agent frameworks, local inference, memory, routing, automation, and security. The library is designed to grow as new research is added.

## Research methodology

Findings distinguish documented behavior from observations, interpretations, and unverified assumptions. Primary sources are preferred, and version-specific details are recorded when available.

Research records follow a consistent structure:

- **Finding:** A specific, source-supported statement.
- **Source:** A publicly accessible reference and identifying details.
- **Context:** The relevant software version, specification, release, or publication.
- **Open questions:** What the available evidence does not establish.
- **Reviewed:** The date the source was inspected.

The presence of a finding does not imply an independent reproduction of the behavior described. Readers should confirm that the cited version and context apply to their use case.

## Dataset

The initial public dataset contains **45 findings across 15 research areas**, exported November 7, 2026. It is a dated snapshot and may not reflect subsequent developments.

The published dataset is readable in JSON and designed for research review, citation, and reuse. Updates to this repository are not currently synchronized automatically with the originating research workspace.
